Do not call a milestone finished because a build is green. For each release candidate, record the **exact final commit** and the **completed success** status of all three `CI` jobs: `build`, `chess-db-integration`, and `chess-legacy-ledger-integration`. Until that job runs, **signed-in employee/client browser and axe acceptance are NOT verified**. The test-only owner/employee/client email and keys remain in canonical owner checklist **01**, not in code.

## Code and acceptance gaps still open - do not mark production ready

1. **M1/M8:** The 58-route CHESS service API inventory is enforced in normal CI, but it does **not** establish all-path sensitive-read logging. Continue full endpoint/function verification beyond the newly audited directories, including legacy document endpoints, provider callbacks, client portals, invoices, calendars and historic statements. Independent privacy/pentest and WORM verification are mandatory.
2. **M3/M5:** The cross-database bank reservation is a **coordination protocol**, not an atomic distributed transaction. A crash between source and tenant commits can still produce `UNKNOWN`, and resolution needs explicit read-only reconciliation, fault-injection and authorized operator intervention. Do not auto-retry an ambiguous financial attempt.
3. **M3:** Real immutable retention, legal-hold release and subject erasure cannot be guessed without counsel and chosen S3/KMS/WORM/e-sign evidence.
4. **M4/M7:** Live worker scheduling, backlogs, paging and operator alerts require staging metrics and on-call escalation; passing local test data is insufficient.
5. **M5/M6/M7:** Real financial settlements, e-file, payroll transfers, statutory accounts and licensed execution depend on signed provider, regulator and professional contracts. Never replace these with fictional success statuses.
6. **M8:** Authenticated two-tenant/two-business browser E2E, accessibility acceptance, operational load/pentest, deployed staging SHA, PITR/WORM restoration, incident pager and signed production launch remain unfinished.
